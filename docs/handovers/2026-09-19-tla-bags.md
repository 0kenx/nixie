### 1. The `Bags` vocabulary, end to end

Before this session every bag operator declined (`unsupported: SetToBag`
×20 in the parity sample). The normative definitions were read from
`tla2tools`' own `Bags.tla` (several surprised me: `BagUnion` takes a *set
of bags*; `SubBag` *enumerates* all subbags; the two-bag union is the
infix `(+)`, lexed as `\oplus`; bags on `1..n` domains print as
sequences). Three layers:

- **Evaluator** (`nixie-tla/src/eval.rs`): the full vocabulary over the
  function representation, routed through the normalising `Value::fun`
  constructor so a bag's spelling is exactly TLC's (a bag on `1..n` prints
  `<<1, 1>>`). Includes the `(-)` positive-part filter, `SubBag`'s
  enumeration, and `\sqsubseteq`'s domain + pointwise reading.
- **Types** (`types.rs`): Snowcat signatures; a bag's surface type is the
  function it is (`Fun(τ, Int)`), so `b[e]` keeps typechecking.
- **Lowering** (`lower.rs::bag_desugar`): the operators desugar to the
  module's *own definitions* as kernel nodes (`SetToBag(S)` →
  `[e ∈ S |-> 1]`, etc.) — the same macro expansion TLA+ gives them, so
  evaluator and encoder see the definition's meaning, not a new primitive.
  `EmptyBag` lowers to its definition instead of a free name. The
  `CHOOSE`/recursion-dependent four (`BagCardinality`'s `Sum`, `SubBag`,
  `BagUnion`, `BagOfAll`) deliberately stay opaque (evaluator-exact,
  encoder-declined by name).
- **BMC** (`nixie-tla-check`): ground construction, symbolic reads
  (`CopiesIn`/`BagIn` over state bags) and ground algebra encode and check
  end-to-end — verified with correct `Violation`/`NoViolationWithin`
  verdicts and independent trace replays. Two honest declines, both naming
  the enumerable-domain wall: **state-bag updates** (`token' = token (-)
  SetToBag({e})` — a `FunDef` over `DOMAIN token`) and quantifiers over a
  symbolic bag's domain. Closing those needs a lambda-shaped function
  encoding or the native `Bag`-sort bridge — documented in
  `bench/tla_bmc/METHODOLOGY.md` (new "Bags" section), not approximated.

One general encoder fix on the way: `collect_unsortable` now collects
`FunDef` nodes — a function over an **empty** domain has no entry to take a
sort from (`EmptyBag` is exactly this shape; the fix serves every
`[x ∈ {} |-> e]`).

### 2. The corpus question, answered honestly

TLA+-lowered specs are **not yet a bag-theory workload**: 7 files in the
corpora mention the bag vocabulary, 1 has the Init/Next/Inv triple
(`Nano.tla`, blocked on **PlusCal** lowering, not on bags), and what bag
usage exists flows through the *function* encoding (per `Bags.tla`'s own
semantics) — nothing in the TLA+ path touches `nixie-solver`'s `Bag`
theory today. Making it do so is the `Bag`-sort bridge (a `sort_of` mapping
for a bag type + encoder representation), which is the next design-sized
step if a bag workload from TLA+ is ever wanted. The bag-theory corpus
question stays open on the solver side; the honest near-term bag workload
remains the differential fuzzers.
