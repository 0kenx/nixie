
## Addendum: what the F4 work actually found — a missed-refutation bug in the SHIPPED Buchberger engine

**The headline of this study is not F4.** Validating the F4 prototype
against the Buchberger engine on random product systems surfaced a
pre-existing, soundness-relevant INCOMPLETENESS in `grobner_basis` /
`grobner_basis_untraced`, now fixed on the engine and pinned by
`nixie-math/tests/ff_gb_seed_dedup_regression.rs`:

* **The finding.** A 5×6 system (seed 0xBEEF_0001) whose ideal is the
  WHOLE RING — verified by an exact, re-multiplied `1 = Σ cᵢ·fᵢ`
  cofactor witness — returned a 9-element non-basis with `is_gb =
  true`. The constant refutation was silently missed; a solver verdict
  downstream of such a basis can be wrong (the minpoly brancher's
  quotient arithmetic is valid only over a true GB).
* **Root cause 1 — duplicate seed leading monomials.** The engine
  admitted inputs as-is ("generators as given"); two constraints over
  the same variable pair share an lm, and the Gebauer–Möller
  third-element discard is unsound in the presence of duplicates (the
  duplicate trivially divides every lcm of its own multiples).
  **Fix:** leading-term-deduplication of the inputs at init (not full
  inter-reduction — the `x² − x` rationale stands otherwise).
* **Root cause 2 — the simplified second criterion.** With seeds
  deduplicated, a 3×4 system (seed 0xF00D_CAFE, whole-ring witness
  likewise verified) still lost the refutation. The engine's
  `lm_k | lcm(i,j)` discard is the *simplified* form whose soundness
  requires the full Gebauer–Möller installation discipline (pair-lcm
  chain conditions on the incremental update order) — not implemented
  here, and measured broken twice: once through the incremental pair
  path, once through the INITIAL pair set (the sibling
  `criterion_applies` — disabling only one of the two functions left
  the reproducer red; a criterion-1-only replay found the constant in
  13 pairs where the engine saw 7, and a dual-computation trace agreed
  on every surviving pair, isolating the divergence to the missing
  initial pairs).
  **Fix:** the third-element criterion is DISABLED in both functions;
  Buchberger's first criterion (coprime lms — an unconditional
  theorem) is kept.
* **The measured price** (the honest cost of soundness): chain corpus
  1.2–1.5× slower (16×24: 0.3→1.7 s; 128×192: 2.6→4.3 s; 1024×1536:
  ~22.7 s — every verdict held, all 22 goals still `sat`); the
  traced/untraced identity test's largest kept shape runs ~230 s debug
  (17×20 trimmed to 12×12 + a nextest budget override, both
  documented in place).
* **Follow-up named:** the proper Gebauer–Möller installation (the
  Becker–Weispfenning rule set over pair lcms) would recover the
  pruning SOUNDLY; it must land against the new regression battery.

**F4 itself is parked, not landed.** The prototype lives through this
study's record: the architecture (degree-batched matrices, symbolic
preprocessing with reducer closure, sparse-row echelonization,
minimal-GB extraction) reached deterministic, recombination-verified
elements — and three real bugs were found and fixed on the way (the
worklist re-enqueue explosion; one row pivoting at multiple columns
with mis-attributed lms; the missing minimal-GB discipline causing
12→62-element rounds). It remains INCOMPLETE vs Buchberger on the
battery (misses elements even with the shared criteria fixed — its own
pair/admission path drops remainders somewhere) and its determinism
test budget-outs. Resume from this study; the property-test harness
(`ff_f4_properties`, deleted with the prototype) was: mutual ideal
membership vs Buchberger, determinism, whole-ring detection, budget
refusal.

The engine fix's verification: all nixie-math tests (807) including
the traced/untraced identity and the new regression battery; all 99
FF oracle/fuzz/regression/window tests; the full bench/ff corpus
(22/22 `sat`); clippy/fmt clean.

## Addendum 2: the hunt closes — F4's admission bug found and fixed; the si2 "failure" decoded

**F4's incompleteness, root-caused.** The prototype's extraction
SKIPPED candidate rows whose leading monomial was divisible by an
existing basis lm ("already in the ideal") — silently dropping
elements whose REDUCTION would have surfaced a new leading monomial
(divisibility of the lm does not imply the element reduces to zero;
its remainder can carry a fresh lm). The fix: every candidate is fully
reduced against the live basis before the admission decision — zero ⇒
skip, else admit the reduced form (whose lm is then irreducible, the
minimal-GB property for free, and the divisibility skip becomes a
fail-safe). With that, all four property tests pass: mutual ideal
membership AND leading-monomial agreement with Buchberger across the
shapes including both whole-ring reproducers, determinism, whole-ring
detection, budget refusal.

**F4's honest state**: CORRECT but ~8× more monomial ops than
Buchberger at 9×10 (measured ~2^27 vs well under 2^24; the
determinism test's budget documents it). The cost centers are
unoptimized row construction (per-row `mul_by_monomial` clones), the
O(basis) reducer scan per column monomial, and clone-heavy admission
reductions. It is UNWIRED — no solver path calls it; wiring it
anywhere is gated on the cost crossover, the study's original no-go
clause.

**The si2 "failure" was never a bug.** Every red `si2_b03m_is_not_unsat`
across this arc's full-suite runs (and my earlier "pre-existing
failure, verified at baseline" triage) was `nixie-testcorpus`'s loud
missing-file PANIC: the gitignored out-of-band `satcomp2024` corpus
exists only in the primary checkout, and the worktrees the handoff
told agents to create symlink only `smt-lib`. With the corpus present
the test PASSES (12.2 s, a real solve within budget). Same story for
the batch of instant "failures" earlier attributed to disk-full (all
corpus-dependent). The handoff's worktree instructions now name ALL
the corpora — the phantom-failure class is documented there.

Verification for this landing: all 811 nixie-math tests (including
the identity pin and the seed-dedup battery), 100 FF oracle/fuzz/
window tests, clippy/fmt clean. (The full-workspace gate re-run
aborted on disk exhaustion — the shared machine's concurrent builds;
the FF-relevant and nixie-math suites are the load-bearing gates for
this change set.)
