## Leg 2 — CNF vs CaDiCaL / Kissat (58 instances)

Timeouts: nixie 26, cadical 25, kissat 24 — but nixie's overlap the others'
solves (WS_500: cadical 47 s; REGRand: kissat 1.9 s).  On jointly-solved
mid-size instances nixie is **2.2–26.6× slower** than the best competitor
(worst: x9-0709 26.6×, nla-dig 26.5×, Break_1 6.5×, bv_ILA 6.1× vs kissat).

## Ranked opportunities

1. **LIA LP bound propagation (structural, biggest payoff).**  wisa2
   signature: nixie 100k decisions / 53 conflicts (search starved of
   conflicts) vs Z3 141 conflicts / 382 decisions with
   `arith-bound-propagations-lp 2370`.  nixie's channel
   (`derive_arith_bound_propagations`) is gated to `QF_UFIDL`
   (`is_dl_family`) because its derived reasons are *incomplete Farkas
   justifications* on dense logics — an unsound propagation clause.  A probe
   (gate hacked open, unlandable) fired the channel on QF_LIA: it converts
   nec-smt `config_read_line` TO→7.3 s `unsat` and the small rings, but
   wisa-class stays TO — the per-assignment + memo shape (3.9 M calls, 388
   props fired once, then the memo blocks everything until an atom is
   asserted) is the wrong shape vs Z3's row-driven propagation at each
   arith check.  **Work item:** row-driven propagation off tableau rows
   with Farkas-complete reason collection — reference Z3
   `smt/theory_arith.*` bound propagation (`assert_bound` → row scan →
   `bound_evidence` with Farkas coefficients), then widen `is_dl_family`
   per-fragment as reasons are proven complete.  This covers most of the
   156-instance QF_LIA gap (wisa, rings, bofill, convert, pb2010,
   c_inference, ezsmt, Dartagnan, miplib, nec-smt, CAV/dillig stragglers).
2. **`ENCODE_DEPTH_LIMIT = 512` honesty-gates deep ite chains.**  nec-smt
   med/* (756-deep ite chains): **0 decisions, instant `unknown`**; Z3
   0.2–1.4 s.  Probe with the limit at 20000: prp-2-26 encodes and searches
   (still TO — compounding with #1).  **Work item:** explicit-stack encode
   for deep chains (the repo's stack-safety principle) instead of a depth
   cap that forfeits the answer; expected to flip the nec-smt
   instant-`unknown` class (1/28 → several/28) even before #1 lands.
3. **CLI SAT-core preset mis-tuning.**  x9-0709: `default` 0.68 s vs
   `aggressive` 0.37 s (1.8×); `conservative`/`industrial`/`cadical`
   1.6–1.8 s; raw `Solver::solve` defaults 0.56 s.  **Work item:** preset
   schedule/auto-selection per instance class (kissat-style phases, or a
   cheap structural probe) and a `default` re-tune against the satcomp
   corpus.
4. **Propagation kernel is memory-bound (CNF leg).**  SIGPROF profile
   (250 Hz, FP build; x9-0709): `scan_list` 56.4 % of samples with
   `propagation_value` (a single unchecked load) 33.6 % — cache-miss
   attribution on the truth array during watch scans; `Trail::reason`
   5.7 %; VSIDS/VMTF heap ops ~7 %.  Consistent with kissat's packed watch
   encodings.  **Work item:** watch-payload layout study against kissat
   `src/watch.h` (packed refs, cache-line grouping), watch-list block
   allocation.

## Infrastructure landed with this scan

- `nixie-cli` optional feature `pprof-signal`: `NIXIE_PPROF=<path.svg>`
  samples the process at 250 Hz and writes a flamegraph at exit.  Works
  around `async fn main`'s frame-pointer breakage for the deep stacks via
  the standalone sync harness pattern (also archived: a 20-line
  `nixie-sat`-linked harness recovers full stacks where the async runtime
  truncates them).

  (native recursion), measured only.  Reverted.
